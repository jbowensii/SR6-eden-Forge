Shadowrun 6th World Catalog Builder
===================================

This turns Shadowrun 6th World PDFs you already own into a compendium module
for Foundry VTT.

NO GAME CONTENT IS INCLUDED. You supply the books; the extraction happens on
this machine and the results stay here. Do not redistribute what it produces.

What you will need:
  * Your Shadowrun 6 PDFs, in a folder
  * Foundry VTT with the shadowrun6-eden system installed
  * Commlink6 (optional) - separate software by Stefan Prelle, from
    rpgframework.de, under his own terms. Not included here.

After installing, launch the Catalog Builder and it will ask four questions,
then walk you through import, review and publishing.
